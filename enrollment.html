How to Enroll

Simple Steps to Become a KCC Student

Step 1: Choose Your Program

Explore the available academic programs and choose the course that matches your interests and goals.

Step 2: Prepare Requirements

Prepare the necessary documents and enrollment requirements before proceeding to the school.

Step 3: Complete Enrollment

Visit the school enrollment office and follow the instructions to complete your registration.

Enrollment Requirements

Prepare Your Documents

For Incoming Freshmen

  • Report Card (Form 138)
  • Certificate of Good Moral Character
  • PSA Birth Certificate
  • Recent 2x2 ID Picture
  • Other documents required by the school

For Transferees

  • Transfer Credentials
  • Transcript of Records (if applicable)
  • Certificate of Good Moral Character
  • PSA Birth Certificate
  • Other documents required by the school

Note: Please confirm the official and updated enrollment requirements with the school before submitting documents.
