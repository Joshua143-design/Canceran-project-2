Admission

Basic Enrollment Requirements

New Students

  • Previous school records
  • Birth certificate
  • Recent ID picture
  • Other documents required by the school

Transferees

  • Transfer credentials or available school records
  • Birth certificate
  • Recent ID picture
  • Other documents required by the school
