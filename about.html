About Our School

Welcome to Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc. Discover our school community, educational environment, and commitment to helping students learn and grow.

Welcome to Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc.

A place where students can discover their abilities, develop their knowledge, and prepare for their future.

Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc. is a school located in Kabankalan City, Negros Occidental. It provides an educational community where students can pursue their academic goals and develop their skills.

Through classroom learning, school activities, and meaningful experiences, students are encouraged to become responsible individuals and lifelong learners.

  • Encouraging learning and personal development
  • Promoting student participation and teamwork
  • Preparing students for future opportunities

Our Direction

Mission and Vision

Mission

KCC commits to provide quality and inclusive Catholic education, rooted in the Gospel and driven by innovative research and meaningful community engagements, empowering its members to become effective agents of societal transformation.

Vision

Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc., as builder of the future, is a Christ-centered learning community of accountable and socially responsible individuals, that leads positive change, responds to and serves the mission of the Diocese, the nation, and the world.
